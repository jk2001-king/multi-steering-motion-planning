Optional installed OMPL geometric metric
--------------------------------------

existing OMPL 1.7 library through a temporary, locally compiled C ABI shim.
OMPL algorithm source, headers, library binaries and generated paths are NOT
bundled in this repository. The installed component retains its own BSD

https://ompl.kavrakilab.org/classompl_1_1base_1_1ReedsSheppStateSpace.html

Algorithm reference: J. A. Reeds and L. A. Shepp, "Optimal paths for a car
that goes both forwards and backwards," Pacific Journal of Mathematics,
145(2):367-393, 1990.
